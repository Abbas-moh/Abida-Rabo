About me

A programme implementation specialist based in Abuja

I have more than ten years in finance and project management at international organisations in Nigeria. My work is setting projects up properly, keeping the money accountable, and getting programmes delivered on time and within budget.

I’ve run Ma Zholkha Global Resources since 2011 and co-founded CFWWomen in 2020. Alongside that, I’ve trained in real estate and investment consultancy, ISO standards, procurement, data analytics and humanitarian response.

What I bring to a team is a steady hand under pressure, a clear read of the numbers, and strong relationships with the partners and stakeholders who share the goal.
